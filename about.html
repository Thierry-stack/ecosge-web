Our Story

From registration to reputation

ECOSG Company Ltd was registered with the Rwanda Development Board on 12 February 2016 as a private company limited by shares, headquartered in Niboye, Kicukiro, in the City of Kigali.

What began as a newly registered contractor has grown into a Category B construction company with a portfolio spanning industrial and factory buildings, cooperative infrastructure, district government offices, bridges, schools, health centres and public markets.

Along the way, our clients have come to include district governments, national institutions such as the Rwanda Biomedical Centre, WASAC and the Rwanda Medical & Dental Council, cooperatives and private companies — each project carried through to completion under the principle behind our name: Until it's done.

What does "Category B" mean?

Rwanda's construction firms are graded into contractor categories based on technical capacity, qualified personnel, equipment and financial strength. This classification determines the scale of construction tenders — public and private — a company is authorised to undertake. As a Category B contractor, ECOSG Company Ltd has met the requirements to design and deliver medium-to-large scale building and civil engineering works, giving clients confidence in our capacity and compliance.
