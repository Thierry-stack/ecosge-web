Who We Are

A Kigali-based contractor built to see every project through

Founded in 2016 and registered with the Rwanda Development Board, ECOSG Company Ltd has grown into a Category B general construction company trusted by district governments, national institutions, cooperatives and private enterprises across Rwanda.

From factory warehouses and cooperative infrastructure to sector offices, health centres, bridges and markets, our team carries every contract from groundbreaking to handover — the commitment behind our name: Until it's done.

  • Registered & certified by the Rwanda Development Board
  • Track record with government, national & private clients
  • Full-scope capability: design consultancy through finishing
